    How ThreadKeep works

    • ThreadKeep only reads a page when you click Export this chat or press Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+E — there is no background or always-on access to your tabs.
    • Your saved chats are stored locally in this browser (chrome.storage.local) and are never sent anywhere.
    • Supported sites with dedicated extraction: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini. Grok, Perplexity and Copilot are supported on a best-effort basis and other pages fall back to exporting your current text selection or the page's main content.
