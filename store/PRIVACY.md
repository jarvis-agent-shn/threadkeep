# ThreadKeep — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-27_

ThreadKeep is private by default.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** ThreadKeep does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
or usage data. No analytics, no trackers, no advertising, no remote servers.

## Where your data lives

Every conversation you save, plus all settings, is stored **locally on your own
device** via the browser's `chrome.storage.local` API. Exported Markdown/JSON
files are written directly to your computer via the browser's download function.
Your data never leaves your device unless you export or share it yourself.

## Permissions, and why

- **storage** — save your library of exported chats and settings locally.
- **downloads** — write the Markdown/JSON export file to your computer when you
  ask to download it.
- **activeTab** and **scripting** — read the conversation on the AI chat page you
  are viewing, only at the moment you click to export it. ThreadKeep uses **no**
  background or always-on content script and requests **no** broad site access
  (`host_permissions`); it reads a page only in direct response to your export
  action, on the tab you are actively using.

## Payments (Pro)

If a paid "Pro" tier is offered, payment is handled by a third-party processor
(ExtensionPay / Stripe) under their own privacy policy. ThreadKeep does not
receive or store your payment details.
